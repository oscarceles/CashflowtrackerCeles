Privacy Policy

Celes Cashflow - Last updated: November 5, 2026

Celes Cashflow ("the App", available at cashflow.celes.ai) is an internal financial reporting tool operated by Celes Technologies, Inc. and its affiliates ("Celes", "we"). This policy explains what data the App accesses, including data from Intuit QuickBooks Online, and how it is used and protected.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by authorized Celes employees and advisors. Access requires signing in with an account that Celes has explicitly authorized. The App is not offered to the general public.

Data we access from QuickBooks Online

When an authorized user connects a QuickBooks Online company, the App uses the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope in read-only fashion to retrieve:

  • Company information (company name).
  • Financial reports: Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss, for the months the user requests.

The App does not create, modify or delete any data in QuickBooks Online, and it does not access payment, payroll or banking credentials.

How we use the data

Report data is used solely to prepare Celes' internal consolidated financial statements and management reports. A user reviews the retrieved figures and decides whether to save them into the App.

Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored server-side in a database table that is not reachable from the browser and is only read by the App's server functions.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. The OAuth flow is protected with a signed, time-limited state value and a secure cookie.
  • Saved financial figures are accessible only to authorized Celes users, based on their assigned role.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with third parties. Data is processed only by the infrastructure providers that host the App (Netlify for hosting and server functions, Supabase for the database), acting on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting QuickBooks from the App revokes the authorization with Intuit and deletes the stored tokens; disconnecting from within QuickBooks invalidates them. Financial figures saved into Celes' internal reports are retained as part of Celes' accounting records. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.
